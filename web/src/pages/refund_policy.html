Refund Policy

Last Updated: May 14, 2026 | Version: 1.0.0

Effective for all subscriptions and trial periods commencing on or after the date listed above.

1. Policy Overview

AEGIS v1.0 offers a customer-friendly refund policy designed to ensure satisfaction with our platform and services. This policy outlines the terms under which refunds may be requested and processed.

Our Commitment: We stand behind the quality of AEGIS v1.0 and want you to be completely satisfied with your subscription.

2. Free Trial Refunds

3-Day Free Trial Period:

  • New users receive a complimentary 3-day trial of AEGIS v1.0
  • No payment information is required to start a free trial
  • The trial automatically ends after 3 days
  • Refund: If a payment method was charged during the trial period in error, a full refund will be issued within 5 business days upon request

⚠️ Important: Trial-only usage does not qualify for refunds. Refunds are only applicable if unauthorized charges were made.

3. Subscription Refunds

3-Day Money-Back Guarantee:

All new paid subscriptions come with a 3-day money-back guarantee from the date of initial payment.

  • Eligible Subscriptions: Monthly and annual paid subscription plans
  • Refund Amount: Full refund of subscription fees (excluding taxes and platform fees)
  • Refund Period: 3 days from the date of payment
  • Processing Time: 5-10 business days after approval

Example: If you paid $99 for a monthly subscription on May 1st, you may request a refund until May 3rd (23:59 UTC).

4. Refund Eligibility

Your request qualifies for a refund if:

  • ✓ You are within the 3-day refund window
  • ✓ You have not already received a refund for this subscription
  • ✓ Your subscription is still active or recently canceled
  • ✓ You provide a valid reason for the refund request

Your request may NOT qualify if:

  • ✗ More than 3 days have passed since payment
  • ✗ You have received a previous refund for this subscription period
  • ✗ You have initiated a chargeback with your payment provider
  • ✗ The refund request lacks proper documentation or justification

5. How to Request a Refund

Step-by-Step Process:

Step 1: Log in to your AEGIS v1.0 account

Step 2: Navigate to Account Settings → Subscriptions → Refund Request

Step 3: Select the subscription for which you wish to request a refund

Step 4: Provide a reason for the refund (e.g., "Service not as expected", "No longer needed", etc.)

Step 5: Submit the form and await confirmation email

Alternatively: Contact our support team at support@aegis.com with your refund request details.

6. Refund Processing Timeline

Stage Timeline Details
Request Submission Immediate Your refund request is logged
Review & Approval 1-2 business days Verification of eligibility criteria
Processing 3-5 business days Refund processed to original payment method
Bank Clearance 2-5 business days Amount appears in your bank account

Total time from request to receipt: 5-12 business days

7. Exceptions and Special Cases

Annual Subscriptions:

  • Annual plans include the 3-day money-back guarantee from purchase date
  • Unused months cannot be refunded after the guarantee period expires
  • Cancellations after the 3-day period will result in loss of unused subscription time

Duplicate Billing:

  • If you were charged twice for the same subscription, the duplicate charge will be immediately refunded
  • No waiting period applies to duplicate billing errors

Fraudulent Transactions:

  • Unauthorized charges will be refunded in full immediately upon verification
  • Identity verification may be required
