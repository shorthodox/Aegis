The Physics of Aegis-1

How we transform market entropy into actionable probability.

1. Ensemble AI (XGBoost)

Aegis-1 doesn't follow a single "strategy." It uses an Extreme Gradient Boosting classifier that optimizes a differentiable loss function. Every 5 minutes, it recalibrates based on 24 technical dimensions.

2. Smart Position Sizing

To avoid the "Risk of Ruin," we suggest a trade size \(S\) using the Aegis-1 Sizing Formula:

$$S = \frac{C \cdot R}{L} \cdot w$$
  • \(C\): Current Capital (Input in Dashboard)
  • \(R\): Risk Percentage (User-defined, e.g., 2%)
  • \(L\): Stop-Loss Distance (Calculated via Volatility)
  • \(w\): AI Confidence Weight (0.5 to 1.0)

3. Sentiment Deceleration

The Emergency Brake isn't a manual switch — it's a VADER-calculated binary gate. If the news sentiment score hits -0.90, the engine overrides the AI and sets trade suggestions to zero.
