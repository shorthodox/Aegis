3. Risk Factors

Market Risk

Market prices can gap beyond stop-loss levels during low liquidity periods, flash crashes, or major news events. Your actual loss may exceed your planned risk.

Execution Risk

Signal generation does not guarantee actual execution at the recommended prices. Slippage, failed transactions, and exchange outages can occur.

Leverage Risk

Using leverage (margin trading) increases both potential profits and losses. A 10% move against your position with 10x leverage results in a 100% loss of your margin. Liquidation can occur without warning.

Technological Risk

AEGIS systems depend on internet connectivity, exchange APIs, and server availability. Technical failures may cause signals to be missed or delayed.

Regulatory Risk

Cryptocurrency regulations are evolving globally. Changes in regulation may restrict your ability to trade or use the platform. AEGIS cannot guarantee compliance with future regulatory changes in your jurisdiction.
