1. All Sales Are Final — No Refunds

AEGIS v1.0 operates on a strict no-refund basis. All subscription payments — monthly, quarterly, or annual — are final and non-refundable once a paid plan is activated.

We can offer this policy fairly because every paid plan is preceded by a free trial that gives you full access to evaluate the service before any money is charged. By upgrading to a paid plan you confirm that you have had the opportunity to evaluate AEGIS during the free trial and that your payment is non-refundable. In particular, no refunds are issued for:

  • Change of mind after subscribing
  • Trading losses incurred while using AEGIS signals (signals are advisory only — you make your own trading decisions)
  • Partially used or unused portions of a billing period after cancellation
  • Dissatisfaction with signal performance, win rate, or the number of signals produced
  • Failure to cancel before an automatic renewal

2. Your Free Trial Is the Evaluation Window

The 72-hour Explorer Trial is completely free and requires no payment method — nothing is charged during the trial. This is your opportunity to review the live signals, the public track record, and every feature of the platform before deciding to pay. Because paid subscriptions are non-refundable, we strongly encourage you to use the full trial to decide whether AEGIS fits your needs.

3. Billing Errors & Unauthorized Charges

The no-refund policy does not apply to genuine billing errors. If you were charged more than once for the same billing period (a duplicate charge), or a charge was made without your authorization, contact us and we will investigate and correct any verified error. This is the only circumstance under which a payment is returned. To report a billing error, email animeshkukreti@gatekeeper.sbs with your account email and transaction ID.

4. Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time from your dashboard. Cancellation stops all future renewals — you will not be charged again — and your access continues until the end of the billing period you have already paid for. No refund, full or partial, is issued for the remaining or unused portion of a billing period.

5. Payment Disputes

Payments are processed securely through Razorpay (UPI, cards, and netbanking). If you believe a charge is incorrect, please contact us first — most issues are resolved quickly and directly. Any statutory rights you may have under applicable consumer law are unaffected by this policy. Filing a chargeback or payment dispute without contacting us first may result in your account being suspended while the matter is reviewed.

6. Contact Us

For any refund inquiries or disputes, contact our support team:

  • Email: animeshkukreti@gatekeeper.sbs
  • Response Time: Within 24–48 hours
