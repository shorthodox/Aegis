Refund Policy

Last Updated: May 11, 2026

1. Refund Eligibility

AEGIS v1.0 offers refunds under the following circumstances:

  • If you request a refund within 7 days of your first subscription payment, you are eligible for a full refund, provided you have not accessed the Pro or Basic plan features beyond initial setup.
  • If the service is unavailable for more than 48 consecutive hours due to our infrastructure failure, you are entitled to a pro-rata refund for the affected period.
  • If the subscription was charged in error (duplicate charges, unauthorized transactions), a full refund will be issued.

2. Trial Refund Policy

The 72-hour Explorer Trial is completely free and does not require a refund process. No payment method is charged during the trial period.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please:

  • Email support@aegis-trading.io with your account email and transaction ID
  • Provide a clear reason for your refund request
  • Include any relevant screenshots or documentation
  • Allow 5-7 business days for processing

4. Refund Processing Timeline

Once approved, refunds are processed through the original payment method:

  • Credit/Debit Card: 5-10 business days
  • Digital Wallets (Paddle, UPI): 2-3 business days
  • Bank Transfers: 5-7 business days

5. Non-Refundable Situations

The following situations are NOT eligible for refunds:

  • Refund requests made after 7 days of the initial subscription payment
  • Trading losses incurred using AEGIS signals (signals are advisory only)
  • Requests due to user error or misuse of the platform
  • Changes of mind after the 7-day window has passed
  • Cancellation of recurring subscriptions (use the dashboard to cancel future renewals)

6. Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time through your dashboard. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle. No refunds are issued for partially used months, but you will not be charged for future periods.

7. Paddle Payment Disputes

If you believe your payment was processed incorrectly, you can file a dispute directly with Paddle:

  • Log into your Paddle account
  • Navigate to Transaction History
  • Click "Dispute" on the relevant transaction
  • Provide supporting documentation

8. Contact Us

For any refund inquiries or disputes, contact our support team:

  • Email: support@aegis-trading.io
  • Response Time: Within 24-48 hours
