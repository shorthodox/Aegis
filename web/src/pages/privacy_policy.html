Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 14, 2026  ·  Version: 1.0.0

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how AEGIS v1.0 collects, uses, and protects your data.

On this page

1. Data Collection

We collect information necessary to provide our services, including:

  • Account Information: Email, phone number, name, location, and profile photo
  • Trading Data: Trading signals generated, bot settings, portfolio performance metrics
  • Technical Data: IP address, device type, browser information, log data, and API usage patterns
  • Behavioral Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent, and interaction patterns
  • Exchange Data: Wallet addresses and trading history (only visible to the user who provides it)

2. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data for:

  • Service Delivery: Generating trading signals, optimizing model performance, personalizing recommendations
  • Improvement: Analyzing usage patterns, debugging issues, enhancing features
  • Communication: Sending updates, support responses, and important notifications
  • Compliance: Meeting legal obligations, fraud prevention, and account security
  • Analytics: Understanding user behavior to improve the platform (anonymized data only)

3. Data Protection & Security

We employ industry-standard security measures:

  • End-to-end encryption for sensitive data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Secure authentication with multi-factor support
  • Restricted access to personal data (need-to-know basis)
  • Compliance with international data protection standards
Note: While we implement strong security, no online platform is 100% secure. Use strong passwords and enable 2FA for your account.

4. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time
  • Portability: Export your data in a standard format
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke consent for optional data processing

5. Third-Party Services

We may share data with trusted partners:

  • Cryptocurrency Exchanges: Read-only access to verify trading data (via public APIs only)
  • Cloud Providers: Firebase, Google Cloud for infrastructure and data storage
  • Analytics Partners: Anonymized usage data for improving platform performance
  • Legal/Compliance: Data shared only when required by law or court order

We do NOT sell personal data to third parties. We do not share private keys, wallet credentials, or API keys with anyone.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. Material changes will be communicated via email or prominent notice on the platform. Your continued use of AEGIS v1.0 constitutes acceptance of any updates.

By using the Platform, you consent to the collection and processing of your personal data as described in this policy.
