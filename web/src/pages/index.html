The Core Differentiator

63 coins.
63 models.

Most signal bots run one universal model for every coin. AEGIS runs sixty-three — one trained per token, on that token's own history and behaviour.

One model per token — not one model for all

BTC and FLOKI have different volatility profiles, different liquidity regimes, different participant behaviours. A model trained on BTC data is the wrong tool for a micro-cap. AEGIS trains a separate XGBoost classifier for each of the 63 tokens it monitors, on that token's own 7,000+ hours of OHLCV history, with its own feature set pruned by SHAP importance. Each token gets the model it deserves.

Meta-labeling: the quant technique behind the filter

After the primary model says BUY, a second model — the meta model — asks: is this a good BUY? It's trained on the primary model's out-of-fold predictions, so it learns to distinguish high-conviction setups from lucky calls. The technique is used by systematic quant funds to cut false positives without sacrificing true ones. AEGIS applies it per token, per side (buy and sell separately).

27-regime adaptive thresholds

The confidence bar for a BUY in "high volatility, downtrend" is higher than in "low volatility, uptrend." AEGIS models this with 27 market buckets (3 volume tiers × 3 volatility tiers × 3 trend tiers) and auto-adjusts thresholds per-regime, per-token, on a rolling out-of-sample window. No manual tuning. No fixed numbers written once and forgotten.

Under The Hood

How a signal is built.

Two tiers of gates separate a candidate signal from a live one. Critical gates veto outright — regime, macro trend, model quality, structure location, risk-reward. Advisory gates then flag anything questionable, and because AEGIS publishes only low- and moderate-risk signals, a single unresolved warning is enough to hold a setup back. Most candidates never make it through. The engine stays quiet when conditions are poor.

01
Market Regime Detection

Before any signal is evaluated, AEGIS classifies the market regime — trending, ranging, volatile, accumulation, or distribution — using a rule-based classifier sharpened by a probabilistic HMM layer. Hard vetoes fire here: no LONG in a confirmed bear regime, no SHORT in a confirmed bull regime, and nothing at all in liquidity traps or when the weekly and daily macro trends both oppose the direction.

02
XGBoost Ensemble Scoring

An XGBoost model trained on 70+ structural features — OHLCV, EMA slopes, RSI momentum, volume Z-score, ATR normalisation, VWAP deviation, funding rate alignment, and more — produces a directional probability and an edge score. Edge scores below 70 are rejected outright, and the floor rises automatically when a model's live win rate drifts below its training benchmark.

03
Structure & Candle Confirmation

Location matters as much as direction. AEGIS only takes entries at structure — a BUY at support, a SELL at resistance — never mid-range. Every entry must prove the turn is already underway on the lower timeframes: at least three of the last four closed 5-minute candles and both of the last two 15-minute candles moving in the signal's direction, with price having actually tested the level (no shorting into empty space below resistance). Breakout entries are taken only after a level has broken and held a retest — never on the run-up into it. If that confirmation can't be verified, the signal is held, not guessed.

04
Quality, Momentum & Volume Confirmation

An advisory tier scores the signal's environment: ADX strength, volume confirmation, regime confidence, macro bias alignment, fake-breakout heuristics and RSI exhaustion — no BUY at momentum peaks, no SELL at capitulation lows. On top of that, a confirmation layer reads the 1-hour chart for Break-of-Structure / Change-of-Character, RSI and MACD divergence, and volume climax + absorption. When structure, momentum and volume all point against the trade, it's held. Every warning a published signal does carry is shown to you.

05
Risk, Reward & Targets

The final check is economic: reward-to-risk must be at least 2:1, measured to a full-trend target. Because every signal is an independent recommendation — not positions in one shared book — AEGIS surfaces opportunities across all 63 tokens at once, with each token holding at most one open signal at a time. Stop-loss and five take-profit levels are then set from real current ATRATR-scaled targets
Exit levels are set by ATR multiples, not fixed percentages — so targets adapt to real current volatility, not historical averages., with the trailing take-profit compressed in high volatility so it's reached within a realistic swing instead of round-tripping to break-even.

Strategy Over Prediction

Trading is a game of strategies, not predictions. Every signal is a systematic execution of risk-to-reward parameters — not a guaranteed outcome. Follow the process.

The Bleed of Fees

Exchange fees and slippage silently erode capital over time. Always account for transactional friction before sizing a position.

Model Variance

Even at 70–80% accuracy, some trades will lose. Statistical expectancy means variance is expected — never risk capital you cannot afford to lose.
