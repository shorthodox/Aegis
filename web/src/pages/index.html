Before You Trade

Trading Reality Check

Strategy Over Prediction

Trading is a game of strategies, not predictions. Every signal is a systematic execution of risk-to-reward parameters — not a guaranteed outcome. Follow the process.

The Bleed of Fees

Exchange fees and slippage silently erode capital over time. Always account for transactional friction before sizing a position.

Model Variance

Even at 70–80% accuracy, some trades will lose. Statistical expectancy means variance is expected — never risk capital you cannot afford to lose.
