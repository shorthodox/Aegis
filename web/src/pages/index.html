How It Works — Every Signal Passes 8 Layers

Before a BUY or SELL signal ever reaches your screen, the engine runs through a strict checklist. Most signals fail this checklist — and that’s exactly the point. Fewer signals, much higher quality.

🧠

AI Per Token

Each of 60 tokens has its own XGBoost model trained on 7,000+ hours of data. A second “meta model” gates the first — a signal only fires if meta-confidence exceeds a per-token precision threshold. This is the technique institutional quant funds use.

📍

S/R + Pattern Gate

Price must be within 1% of a structural support or resistance level. Then reversal patterns (RSI divergence, hammer candles, engulfing) add to a score. Only signals above the threshold advance. Mid-range noise is killed at this layer.

📈

Positive EV Required

Before any signal fires, the engine computes: AI_prob × TP% − (1−AI_prob) × SL% − fees. If the expected value is negative, the signal is killed. No losing-expectancy trades reach your screen.

₿

Macro + BTC Brake

Two final guards: if the macro trend is strongly bearish, BUY signals are blocked across all tokens. If BTC drops >2% below its EMA-200, all BUY signals are shut off. It respects the bigger picture.

The Strategy — Dual-Regime Quantitative Engine

Aegis-1 runs a Hybrid Dual-Regime Quantitative Strategy — a systematic combination of two approaches that most retail tools can only pick one of. The AI acts as the referee, detecting which regime is active in real time and routing the signal through the right path automatically. No manual switching. No guessing.

Primary Path

Structural Mean Reversion

When it activates: Price is within 1% of a support or resistance level and the market isn't in a strong trend.

What it does: Waits for exhaustion evidence at the level — RSI divergence, reversal candles — then waits for 3 confirming candles, then checks expected value. Only then does it signal.

In simple terms: price bounces off a floor or rejects a ceiling — the bot waits for proof that the bounce is real before acting.

Secondary Path

Algorithmic Momentum Breakout

When it activates: The Efficiency Ratio exceeds 0.50 — meaning price is moving directionally with purpose, not oscillating in a range.

What it does: Bypasses the reversal filter stack entirely. If resistance is already broken and the AI confirms momentum, the signal fires immediately as MOMENTUM_BREAKOUT.

In simple terms: when the market is in a strong move, waiting for a reversal means missing the whole trade. The bot chases it instead.

For Experienced Traders — Why the Strategy Is Architecture-Locked

The engine is multi-layered within its own strategy family — regime detection, timeframe stacking (1m through 4h, plus macro 1D/1W), confluence weighting, and sequential confirmation all work as one unified system, not interchangeable modules.

The XGBoost models were trained on a specific feature set: ATR volatility bands, volume moving averages, BTC proximity matrices, Efficiency Ratio, and S/R distance metrics. A different strategy would require different features — and the models would produce noise. Think of it like a prop firm's systematic book: the model, the features, and the rules are one unified system. Changing one without retraining the rest degrades the whole.

What it does offer is adaptability to market condition, not strategy type. Trending, ranging, and volatile markets all route to the correct confirmation path automatically. That's the architectural flexibility — not openness to arbitrary strategies, but robustness across the full range of conditions those strategies typically fail in.
