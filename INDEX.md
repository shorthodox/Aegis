## 📊 Impact Summary

### Root Cause Ranking
1. **Meta gate anti-selectivity** (-12.6pp measured) ← **PRIMARY FIX**
   - Gate rejects winners (50% precision) and accepts losers (37.4% precision)
   - Caused by hold pollution (66% HOLD labels in meta training)
   - Fixed by: C_excluded strategy (exclude HOLD bars)

2. **Hold pollution** (-5pp estimated)
   - Current strategy A_current treats HOLD as zero-target
   - Best strategy C_excluded excludes HOLD entirely
   - Improvement: +0.62 Sharpe, +3pp precision

3. **Feature drift** (-2pp estimated)
   - Secondary factor, lower impact than meta gate
   - Can be addressed with feature normalization

4. **Regime policy mismatch** (-0pp)
   - NOT a factor for SOL (all regimes enabled)
   - BTC also has regime blocking yet maintains 66% precision
